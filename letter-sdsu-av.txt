Dear Hiring Committee,

I'm applying for the Theatrical Lighting, Projection, and Audio Specialist position with the School of Theatre, Television, and Film. Sound has been my craft for over fifteen years. I taught myself production as a teenager and released my first record at eighteen. Since then I've produced and mastered albums, toured venues in Belgium, and run live audio for a weekly comedy show in Los Angeles that I helped create. These days I'm producing a 13-track album for a client artist in my own studio. Audio is what I've done my whole adult life.

The part of this job that gets me is the teaching. My favorite moments in production have always been walking an artist from a rough idea to a finished track they're proud of. Getting to do that with students learning sound, lighting, and projection sounds like a job I'd look forward to on Monday mornings. The coordination side I already live: I run the budgets, purchasing, and equipment inventory for my studio, and before that I spent two years in warehouses where nothing left the building unless the paperwork matched.

I want to be straight about one thing. My depth is in audio. My lighting and projection knowledge comes from film school and from set experience on HBO and Apple productions, not from years in a theater shop. What I can promise is that I learn technical systems fast, because that's how I built everything I have, and I'd come in hungry to master the theatrical side under your productions.

I'm local, I can start immediately, and the campus schedule works well for my family, with room for production calls when shows need them.

Thank you for your consideration,
Anthony Magdalena
